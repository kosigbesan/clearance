How to use it

  1. Drop the finished cutThe scan reads the audio on your own machine and finds every place music plays.
  2. Look at the cuesEach cue gets a timecode and a still from the scene, so you can see which sound is which.
  3. Declare each oneIn the cue sheet, say where the sound came from and what proof you hold. The risk falls out of that.
  4. Run the Shield stepsHash the file, register your score, upload private, read the Copyright column, then publish.
  5. Keep the packCertificate, cue sheet and hash. That is what turns a claim into an afternoon instead of a takedown.

The cue sheet

Every music cue in the cut, in order. The route and the proof decide the risk, not the length of the cue. If a route is unknown, the tool assumes the worst, because that is what YouTube's matcher does.

Triage the editor's sound folder

Paste the listing of the folder your editor cut from, one file per line. Filenames give away downloads that never had a licence, and this is where the film that prompted this tool went wrong. Run it in the folder with dir /s /b > sounds.txt, then paste the contents.
